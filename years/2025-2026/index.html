7 November 2025

What happens in the boundary layer?

Ryan Russell

What happens in the boundary layer for solutions to the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations, asymptotically for small viscosity (high Reynolds number), is not generally known. Heuristic and numerical methods have been proposed and, in special circumstances, verified. I will discuss aspects of the history of this problem, and demonstrate a connection between the work of Chorin (with Marsden and others) in the 1970s, Kato in the 1980s, and Maekawa and others over the past decade or so. This talk is based on joint work with Prof. Jim Kelliher.

17 October 2025

On the hyperkahler nature of the fundamental group

Mo Vaezimonfared

The fundamental group is one of the most basic topological invariants of a space. However, it also encodes a list of flat structures. Hitchin, in his seminal work, by studying a special class of solutions of the self-dual Yang-Mills equations, showed the correspondence between the space of solutions and the moduli space of certain holomorphic/harmonic bundles, called Higgs bundles. On the other side of story, The correspondence between representations and Higgs bundles can be viewed as a Hodge theorem for non-abelian cohomology. This powerful machinery was later extended by Simpson to give a uniformization theorem for kahler manifolds.

Brownian Motion in Financial Markets

Andrea Abeje-Stine

I derive Brownian motion as a scaling limit of a random walk, the Black-Scholes model from Ito's Lemma, discuss Donsker's Invariance Principle applied to financial markets, and speculate about theoretical approaches to studying market microstructure. That said, I couldn't decide what to talk about so the topic is tentative; every talk I give is improvised so I am always happy to change topics per audience request. Please ask questions and heckle me. Topics I can talk about: My dissertation research on a categorical framework for classical mechanical physics; a theory for a co-inclusion exclusion principle and how that shows up in my dissertation work and in reinforcement learning problems; how to get a job in quant finance (except I don't have one yet so what do I know).
